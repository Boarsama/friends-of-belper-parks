Why we exist

Healthy green spaces benefit wildlife, local identity and everyday wellbeing. Our purpose is to encourage their responsible care, share knowledge about their natural and historic value, and help build a lasting connection between people and place.

Our focus is grounded in the Belper area and the wider landscape of Duffield Frith. We support practical, appropriate work that respects landowners, public authorities and the ecological needs of each site.

Belper Parks

Belper Parks is an area of woodland and grassland close to Belper town centre. It once formed part of the Royal Forest of Duffield Frith and was designated a Local Nature Reserve in 2004 through partnership work involving Amber Valley Borough Council, Groundwork Trust and Friends of Belper Parks.
