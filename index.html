Our purpose

Practical care for places that matter.

We believe local green spaces are part of a community’s living heritage. Our purpose is to encourage responsible stewardship, strengthen habitats and help people feel connected to the landscapes around them.

01

Nature & habitat

Supporting thoughtful work that protects biodiversity and gives native plants and wildlife room to flourish.

02

Places & access

Encouraging safe, welcoming and well-understood green spaces that can be enjoyed by the wider community.

03

Heritage & learning

Helping keep the stories, ecology and character of Belper’s historic landscape visible for future generations.

“A local nature reserve is more than a place on a map — it is a shared promise to the future.”

A landscape with a story

Belper Parks belongs to a much older landscape.

The reserve includes woodland and grassland close to the centre of Belper. The area formed part of the medieval Royal Forest of Duffield Frith and was designated a Local Nature Reserve in 2004.

Our organisation exists to support respectful, community-minded care for this distinctive local environment and related green spaces.
