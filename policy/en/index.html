In-app privacy

HACHI Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 25 August 2026

In brief: HACHI requires no account and does not track people across apps. The current beta makes no ad request because the commercial advertising gate remains off. Optional Firebase Analytics is off by default and starts only after explicit consent. HACHI's care journey, notes, Plus experiment answers and health-adjacent answers remain on your device. The open beta may unlock the complete HACHI Plus preview free of charge; this is not a purchase or permanent entitlement. Google Play processes a purchase only if that product is later made available.

1. Publisher and data controller

HACHI is published by FRIENDS OF BELPER PARKS LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 08731873. In this policy, “HACHI”, “we” or “the app” is used as the context requires.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the HACHI Android app. A separate notice for this website is available on the website privacy page. HACHI is a movement and self-care product; it is not a medical device, emergency service or diagnostic service.

3. Data HACHI may store on the device

HACHI stores data only as a result of your explicit use of the app, including:

  • HACHI care and journey state: care dates, movement completion type, selected intent or authored exercise ID, exploration total, opened places, journey memories and today's rest choice;
  • movement choices, training setup, confirmed supports, program progress and structured post-movement feedback;
  • sound, language and local-reminder settings and notification-permission state;
  • First Aid notes you type, and meal-planning, meal-journal or energy observations you explicitly enter and save;
  • structured receipts and decisions needed to prevent duplicates and preserve correct app state.
  • if you start a Plus experiment: one reviewed exercise identifier, a 7- or 14-day calendar window, at most one “lighter”, “same” or “unsure” answer per day, and at most six recent summaries;
  • your Analytics consent choice and, if Plus is made available and purchased, an on-device offline entitlement cache.

This data is held in HACHI's private app storage on the device. HACHI does not create a server-side user profile, and the publisher has no server copy from which to view or restore this data.

4. Data and permissions HACHI does not collect

This release:

  • does not require an account or sign-in;
  • the current beta makes no ad request and does not track people across apps;
  • does not use Analytics for advertising or cross-app tracking;
  • does not request camera, photo/video, microphone, location, contacts or body-sensor access;
  • does not read from or write to Health Connect;
  • does not store food photos, camera data, barcodes, GPS data or raw sensor samples;
  • does not send HACHI care records, exercise choices, symptoms, safety answers, First Aid notes, meal records, calorie values, journey memories, Plus experiment answers, purchase tokens or free text to Firebase Analytics.

Google Play and the Android operating system may separately process technical data to distribute, protect and update the app under their own policies. HACHI does not receive your Google password or payment credentials.

5. How the data is used

Local data is used only to:

  • show accurate progress, choices, memories and places opened with HACHI;
  • run the movement or content you selected;
  • prevent duplicate records and preserve state when the app is closed and reopened;
  • show local history, reminders or suggestions you enabled;
  • summarise your own bounded Plus experiment observations without claiming a health effect;
  • let you view, change or delete your data.

HACHI does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

6. Optional Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics is disabled by default. It starts only if you explicitly opt in from HACHI. HACHI then sends a small, fixed vocabulary of parameter-free product milestones, such as opening a core surface or starting the one-minute flow. HACHI sets no Analytics user ID, disables Advertising ID collection and advertising-personalisation signals, and does not use Analytics for advertising.

Google Analytics for Firebase may also process basic app, device and session events and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier as a service provider. You can withdraw consent in Settings. HACHI then disables new collection and resets Analytics data held on the device. Data already delivered to Google is handled under Google's Analytics retention and deletion controls.

7. Advertising in a future commercial release

The commercial advertising gate remains off in the current beta. If it is enabled in a separately reviewed release, free users may see at most one passive banner at the end of Journey. HACHI places no ad on Today, Plan, check-in, recovery, active exercise, completion/result screens or Pick a card/Tarot, and uses no app-open, interstitial or mandatory rewarded ad. Verified HACHI Plus entitlement removes this banner.

The banner is delivered by Google Mobile Ads. Google may process an IP address, device or app identifiers, diagnostics and ad interactions to deliver, protect and measure advertising under its terms and privacy policy. HACHI requests contextual, non-personalised ads only and does not request the Android Advertising ID or Android AdServices advertising permissions. Where required, HACHI uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain consent before making an ad request and to let users revisit their privacy choices.

HACHI does not put energy level, symptoms, safety answers, movement or recovery choices, meals, weight, sleep, notes, memories, care history, Plus experiments or Tarot cards into an ad request field and does not use them to select advertising.

8. Optional HACHI Plus purchase

If HACHI Plus is made available, it is a one-time, non-consumable digital product handled by Google Play. Google processes payment and restoration under its own terms and privacy policy. HACHI does not store card details, payment amount, order ID or purchase token. It may keep a boolean entitlement cache in private device storage so an already-owned local feature can work offline.

HACHI Plus purchasing is disabled in open-beta builds until the release has completed Play licensing, refund and revocation verification. During open beta, the complete Plus preview may be available free of charge. Preview access is not a purchase, permanent ownership or proof that the product is available for sale.

9. Retention and deletion

  • dated HACHI care records: up to 45 days;
  • Previous Meal feedback: up to 28 days;
  • user-chosen movement receipts: up to 30 days;
  • an active Plus experiment remains until its 7- or 14-day window ends or you stop it; at most six recent experiment summaries are retained;
  • other dated journals and decisions: generally up to 90 days;
  • current state such as settings, profiles, journey step total and opened places stays until you change or delete the related data.

HACHI retains one minimal technical reset boundary so old records cannot reconstruct a journey you deleted. It contains no exercise, duration, intent or care-day fact and never counts as progress.

You can delete individual data groups from HACHI's Settings. To erase all app data, use Android Settings > Apps > HACHI > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall HACHI. Because there is no server copy, the publisher cannot restore data after it is deleted.

10. Sharing, service providers, clipboard and external links

HACHI does not sell local data or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Google acts as a service provider only for optional consented Analytics, Google Mobile Ads in the future commercial release described above and, when available, a user-initiated Google Play purchase. If a feature offers a clipboard export, nothing is copied until you explicitly activate that control; subsequent clipboard use is controlled by you and the operating system.

If you choose to open a support directory or another external page, HACHI hands the link to your web browser. The external website's policy applies from that point. HACHI does not receive the content you view there.

HACHI opens the Android Sharesheet only after you explicitly tap a share action. Generic invitation copy contains no exercise, movement purpose, symptom, safety answer, note, care date or path memory. If you share a Three-Leaf Week postcard, it contains only generic event artwork and a 0–3 leaf count. The PNG is generated and cached temporarily on-device so Android can hand it to the app and recipient you select; HACHI does not upload the image or receive that selection.

11. Security

HACHI uses Android private app storage and disables Android Auto Backup for app data. Its read, write and deletion flows are designed not to invent data when storage operations fail. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure, however. Protect your device with a screen lock and current security updates.

12. Children

HACHI is designed for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data through the app.

13. Changes to this policy

If HACHI's data practices change, we will update this page and its “last updated” date. Material changes will also be reflected in the app or release information where appropriate.
